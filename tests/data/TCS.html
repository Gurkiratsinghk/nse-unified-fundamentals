TCS Stock Price Analysis and Quick Research Report. Is TCS an attractive stock to invest in?

Stock investing requires careful analysis of financial data to determine a company's true net worth. This is generally done by examining the company's profit and loss account, balance sheet and cash flow statement, which can be time-consuming and cumbersome.

Examining a company's financial ratios is an easier way to determine its performance, which can help to make sense of the overwhelming amount of information in its financial statements. 

Here are a few indispensable ratios that should be a part of every investor’s research process, or, in simpler words, how to analyse TCS. 

  • PE ratio: Price to Earnings ratio, which indicates how much an investor is willing to pay for a share for every rupee of earnings. A general rule of thumb is that shares trading at a low P/E are undervalued (it depends on other factors too). TCS has a PE ratio of 18.0970699209999 which is high and comparatively overvalued.

  • Return on Assets (ROA): Return on Assets measures how effectively a company can earn a return on its investment in assets. In other words, ROA shows how efficiently a company can convert the money used to purchase assets into net income or profits. TCS has ROA of 35.5838% which is a good sign for future performance. (Higher values are always desirable.)

  • Current ratio: The current ratio measures a company's ability to pay its short-term liabilities with its short-term assets. A higher current ratio is desirable so that the company could be stable to unexpected bumps in business and economy. TCS has a Current ratio of 2.0064.

  • Return on equity: ROE measures the ability of a firm to generate profits from its shareholders' investments in the company. In other words, the return on equity ratio shows how much profit each rupee of common stockholders’ equity generates. TCS has a ROE of 61.5535%. (Higher is better)

  • Debt to equity ratio: It is a good metric to check out the capital structure along with its performance. TCS has a Debt to Equity ratio of 0 which means that the company has low proportion of debt in its capital.

  • Sales growth: TCS has reported revenue growth of 2.8322% which is poor in relation to its growth and performance. 

  • Operating Margin: This will tell you about the operational efficiency of the company. The operating margin of TCS for the current financial year is 27.9386072110728%.

  • Dividend Yield: It tells us how much dividend we will receive in relation to the price of the stock. The current year dividend for TCS is Rs 110 and the yield is 4.4995%.

  • Earnings Per Share: It tells us how much profit is allocated to to each outstanding share of a common stock. The latest EPS of TCS is Rs 135.696. The higher the EPS, the better it is for investors.

About TCS (Tata Consultancy Services)

Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) is the flagship company of the illustrious Tata Group and a global leader in IT services, consulting, and business solutions. Established in 1968, TCS has grown to become India's largest IT company by market capitalisation and one of the most valuable IT service brands worldwide.

Headquartered in Mumbai, TCS operates in over 50 countries and employs more than 600,000 consultants. It is a critical pillar of the Tata Group, contributing significantly to the group's overall revenue and market value. Known for its execution excellence and long-term client relationships, TCS partners with some of the world's largest businesses to drive their digital transformation journeys.

The Tata Group Connection

TCS is the crown jewel of the Tata Group, India's largest multinational business conglomerate. Tata Sons, the principal investment holding company of the Tata Group, holds the majority stake (over 72%) in TCS. This strong parentage provides TCS with:

  • Stability & Trust: The "Tata" brand is synonymous with trust and ethical business practices globally.

  • Strategic Support: Access to the Tata Group's vast network and resources.

  • Dividends: TCS is a consistent cash cow for Tata Sons, funding the group's philanthropic activities and investments in new ventures.

TCS Business Model

TCS operates on a customer-centric "Global Network Delivery Model" (GNDM™), ensuring seamless service delivery across borders. Its revenue streams are diversified across multiple verticals:

  1. BFSI (Banking, Financial Services, and Insurance): The largest revenue contributor, providing core banking solutions, digital payments, and risk management systems to global banks and insurers.

  2. Retail & CPG: Helping major retailers with e-commerce platforms, supply chain optimisation, and customer experience management.

  3. Life Sciences & Healthcare: Delivering drug discovery platforms and patient care solutions.

  4. Manufacturing: Supporting smart factories, IoT (Internet of Things), and supply chain digitisation.

  5. Products & Platforms: Revenue from proprietary software products like TCS BaNCS (banking suite) and ignio™ (cognitive automation).
