Bajaj Finance Share Price, Stock Analysis & Company Profile 

Bajaj Finance Limited is a dominant force in India’s non-banking financial sector and a core subsidiary of Bajaj Finserv. As one of the most diversified NBFCs in the country, it has transitioned from a captive financier for Bajaj Auto to a financial powerhouse. This page serves as a comprehensive hub for investors seeking an in-depth Bajaj Finance stock analysis, providing all the essential tools to track the Bajaj Finance share price and evaluate its long-term growth potential.

About Bajaj Finance

Founded originally as Bajaj Auto Finance in 1987, the company has evolved into a technology-driven financial services giant. Headquartered in Pune, it caters to millions of customers across India through a massive network of rural and urban lending points.

Bajaj Finance is renowned for creating the "no-cost EMI" ecosystem in India, which revolutionised consumer durable financing. Today, it holds a significant market share in consumer finance, MSME lending, and commercial lending, consistently maintaining high asset quality and robust risk management frameworks.

Bajaj Finance Business Model

The business model of Bajaj Finance is built on the pillars of "Cross-sell, Upsell, and Technology." It operates through a highly diversified portfolio:

  • Consumer Lending: This includes financing for smartphones, laptops, home appliances, and furniture. It also covers personal loans and credit card distribution.

  • SME Lending: Providing working capital loans, professional loans for doctors and CAs, and business loans to small and medium enterprises.

  • Commercial Lending: Focusing on large-scale corporate lending and loan against securities.

  • Rural Lending: A dedicated vertical that brings modern financial products to India’s hinterlands.

  • Mortgages: Through its subsidiary, Bajaj Housing Finance, it offers home loans and loans against property.

  • Deposits: The company accepts public and corporate deposits, holding high credit ratings (AAA) which ensures a low cost of funds.

Bajaj Finance Corporate Action History

Bajaj Finance has a consistent history of rewarding its long-term shareholders through strategic corporate actions that have improved liquidity and increased share count for investors.

Stock Split History
The company has undergone a major stock split to make the shares more affordable for retail investors and improve market liquidity.

  • 2016: Stock Split from a Face Value of Rs 10 to Rs 2.

    • Ex-Date: 09 September 2016

Bonus Share History
In tandem with the stock split, Bajaj Finance issued bonus shares to its investors to further enhance shareholder value.

  • 2016: 1:1 Bonus Issue (1 bonus share for every 1 share held).

    • Ex-Date: 09 September 2016

Investor Note: The 2016 corporate action was a "double delight" for shareholders, combining both a stock split and a bonus issue in the same period, which significantly multiplied the number of shares held by long-term investors.
